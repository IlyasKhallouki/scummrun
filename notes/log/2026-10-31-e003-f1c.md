kitchen's reading stay within 3 times the same switches without it, and does its task plan to the meat?

## Why it grows

All of the growth is in the bar's inputs. Sentences and walks go from 29,968 records to 433,888, of which 400,116 have var:32 = 202.
Events grow by 279 and Esc not at all. One input, sentence(7,320), goes from 44 records to 792:

1. **6 from local 202's tests on the cook** ([0034]-[005B]): actorroom:6 is 28, 72, 0 or not known, and in the room x <= 310 and not
   moving start local 216. They multiply the sentence's own paths, since the record runs both. These are the rule's own forks.
2. **3 from global 4's first test** ([0000]-[0023]): Bit[547] = 0, or Bit[547] = 1 with Var[182] = 0 or above. Var[182] is a fact and no
   constant of the rule. Bit[547] has no write in the game's text and no record of the reading writes it. It is a fact only because
   some Bit writes have an index that does not fold. taskcore's reductions remove those records, which is why the operators grow less
   than the records.

An experiment, not in the branch: with Bit[547] pinned to 0 on the [n] jobs by a hard-coded pin, the kitchen gives 211,537 records, 57.6 s
and 169.2 CPU s against the same hour's base of 26.7 s and 71.4 CPU s (2.2 and 2.4 times), 43,363 operators (2.45 times), and a plan.
With Bit[547] = 1 a left click on the walk verb becomes the hover's default verb ([0023]-[002F]), so it is a state in which the walk's
constant verb does not hold.
