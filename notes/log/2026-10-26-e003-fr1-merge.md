## The conflicts

- The machine's `getter_base`: getActorRoom with actor_rooms reads its fact; findObject and actorFromPos with
  value_sets take their value sets. getActorRoom of an actor that is not known reads as getActorRoom's non-fact,
  and a range of actors counts as not known (`tests/value_sets.rs` `actor_room_of_value_sets`).
- The reader's read hook keeps lane A's split (`read_fact` and the actor-room wrapper) with lane B's value-set forks
  and mouse ranges inside `read_fact`.
- The reader had two switch types. It now has one, `builder::Switches` (due_first, value_sets, draw_clears,
  actor_rooms). The scope and the domains take F5's and F9's (`Switches::scope`); value_sets reaches the machine
  through the table, as before. `Reader::sync` copies only F5's and F9's switches, so it does not undo value_sets.
- The two reader test files are one; builder.rs and the reader's port notes keep both sides.
