## What blocked, in order, and what each fix was

Each block was found by running, diagnosed from the archive or a stack, and fixed by a general rule behind a switch,
with a test. Most were defects in earlier experiments' code that the new paths reached for the first time.

1. **No game files in the new worktree.** The first Monkey Island check timed out on every job (1 h 40 min).
2. **The reader could not read back the machine's "widened loop" reason.** The first try panicked.
3. **Unsetting scope growth gave the guide's 8-room ring, not the room.** The first reading could not finish within
   its bound. Room scopes became a value of the switch.
4. **One lock over every reading's planner, and plans of up to 600 s.** One long search stopped all ten workers for
   minutes at a time. Each planner now has its own lock, the first plans are limited to 10 s and every plan to 30 s.
   Successful plans had taken 0.1 s at the median and 3.5 s at the p95.
5. **What a try keeps.** Single-atom novelty cannot see "a known room, entered holding a new object". So the four
   tries that walked 946 into room 63 kept nothing. The place, a room with the objects ego owns, became a novelty
   unit.
6. **The selection never returned to room 69.** The 946 states were all there, and room 49's conversation kept making
   untried cells. The selection now orders places by their own unproductive tries before cells. Within minutes, 946
   was held in rooms 63 and 49: that is C4.
7. **Realize treated a held object as still in the room.** ScummVM's pickup leaves the object in the room's table, so
   realize tried to approach 946 instead of using it from the inventory.
8. **A reused reading could never reach a cleared bit.** Engine facts drop zeros, and the reach extension's start
   carried only the keys present, so every model try from the conversation's deep states was refused. That was 137
   such stops before the fix and 8 after (all script locals).
