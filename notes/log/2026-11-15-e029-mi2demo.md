# 2026-11-15: E029, the direct search on the Monkey Island 2 DOS demo (SCUMM v5)

Question: how much of the Monkey Island 2 DOS demo (ScummVM `monkey2` "Demo", MI2DEMO.000 9,770 bytes, md5
387a544b..., the only MI2 demo in ScummVM's tables) does E027's search reach from the boot?

No plan was written before the run: the PI asked for it as fast as possible, on the VM. The prediction given to him
beforehand (in conversation): 10 to 15 minutes of search.

## What the demo is

The non-interactive demo: the original plays back DEMO.REC, a recorded session that jumps rooms with the SPUTM
debugger. ScummVM replays it (`Playback`, playback.cpp) and has a setting, `disable_mi2_ni_demo`, that turns the
playback off ("expect frequent crashes"). The demo's files hold 25 of the game's 102 rooms (LOFF of MI2DEMO.001: 17;
MI2DEMO.002: 8).

- Playback on (as installed): the search's boot is idle at frame 107 in room 7; 5 stops, 426 trials, then nothing new.
- Playback off: the boot script loads room 5, which the demo does not hold: `Unknown res tag 'LECF' ... (Room,5)`,
  an engine error at frame 93, before any idle frame.
- Playback off and Esc at frame 3 (a free run): the intro's override is taken, room 7 at frame 3, idle from frame 17,
  no error in 3,333 frames. The bridge presses skips only after the segment start (the first idle), so the search
  never pressed it.

## The changes

- `FMI_GAME_INI` (the search, fmi-engine host.rs, commit 0993580): more of the game's ScummVM settings, K=V,..;
  unset, none.
- Patch 0013 (`scumm-speedrun-skip-early`, commit 5380552): the game's setting `speedrun_skip_early` lets the skips
  be pressed before the segment start too, still only on a frame where a press has an effect. Unset, as before.
- Gate: MI's boot reference (bench.sh, 2 threads, 7 stops) gives 2,219 trials, 52 kept, digest 644e9e311c with high6i
  on svm-e027 and with high6i on svm-e028 + 0013, as with high6 before. Only the boot reference was run, not the
  five references and the demo's 25 stops: both changes act only through a setting MI does not have.

Laptop probe (high6i, svm-e028 + 0013, 3 threads, 2 minutes): boot idle at frame 15 in room 7; 9 rooms and level 4.

## mi2d1 (VM, n2-custom-30-49152, 28 threads, full flag set, no guide, no rungs, no scripts dump, `--stall 15`)

Engine on the VM: its svm-e027 with 0013 applied (0012 is v2 only). Settings:
`FMI_GAME_INI=disable_mi2_ni_demo=true,speedrun_skip_early=true`.

- Boot: frame 15, room 7, idle.
- Levels (objects owned, first at s): 1 at 0.5, 4 at 12.6, 8 at 47.2, 11 at 107.8, 12 at 389.4; none after.
- Rooms: 7, 2, 4, 9, 15, 8, 14, 11, 12 in the first 7 s; 13 at 102 s. Ten of the 25 the files hold.

  the like).
- The top stop (2263, room 8, level 12) replays from the boot: frame 8,921, 185 clicks (`verify`, ok).
- The rooms the game asked for that the demo does not hold (the engines' error messages): 5 (180 times), 23 (80),
  16 (76), 20 (76). These are the ways out of the rooms reached that lead nowhere in the demo.
- The 15 rooms held and not reached (3, 18, 27, 29-32, 46-48, 51, 52, 71, 87, 93): no click of the search led to
