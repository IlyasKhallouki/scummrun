## Surprises

- **The wait decides the kitchen.** r11 saw every object of room 28 and never entered room 41. The door opens on the
  cook's timer, so a click on it works only after a wait. Keeping the waited state was not enough either: by the
  time it was kept, its atoms were old where ego stood. Trying the inputs again from the waited state, inside the
  same expansion, is what passed the door.
- **Position needs three things.** The camera's bucket chains floor steps across a wide room; the walk box and the
  visible objects say when a step showed something. With only the last two (r3-r8), no step was ever new.
- **The sentence line follows the pointer.** After a click on an object the line names it, so every state looked new.
  Parking the pointer on a point that is no verb, object or actor removes the text.
- **Half-made states must not hold the world.** In room 52 object states change by themselves. A hash that held them
  made each floor click a new half-made state (126 a round).
- **One state's trials side by side is slow.** s1 ran one state at a time with its trials on 7 lanes and reached a
  quarter of r12's rate. Batches of states (s2 on) keep the order fixed without that cost.
- **A reporting error of mine.** During r11 I named room 29 the kitchen. Room 29 is another room; the kitchen is
  room 41 (`data/scripts-dos-cd/index.json`). r11 did not reach room 41.
