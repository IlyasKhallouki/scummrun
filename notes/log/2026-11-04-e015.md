# 2026-11-04: E015, three gates in the engine on play8's archive

The retrospective made three claims about the demo, each cheap to test offline on play8's archive. E015 tested them
in the engine, with no run, and had each gate checked again from a separate build. Details and every number are in
`experiments/E015-engine-gates/plan.md`, section "Result".

## The item gate: room 42 was off the path, and 946 is the key (pass)

- From exit A, room 63 leads to room 69 with one click on object 854, and room 69 gives object 946 with one sentence
  (verb 11, then 946). The whole chain is 9 clicks from the boot and replays with 0 differences.
- In the scripts, the only start of room 42's local 212 that can run there is in 946's code. It is also the only clear
  of class 12 on object 491.
- In 30,000 sweep trials from play8's room-42 entries, nothing started local 212 or cleared that class.
- The check then showed the positive half: carrying 946 into room 42 on play8's opening branch, the same sentence
  that does nothing without it runs local 212. The 35-click chain replays from the boot.
- One condition is new. Room 42 was reached holding 946 only from one of the opening's choices; from the other,
  three searches did not find it.

## G1: the engine can realize a sentence on its own (pass)

- The planner aimed at exit A 22 times, and every attempt died at its first input, on Monkey Island's walk verb.
- A routine that searches clicks in forks, and accepts the one whose run the engine's own sentence log shows as the
  planned sentence, did it without any interface profile or verb number. That took 2 floor clicks and 1 bare click,
  and room 63 loaded after the dialog choice. The chain replays with 0 differences.
- It costs milliseconds, not seconds: 2-10 ms per sentence.
- Only the simplest candidate (a bare click) was exercised on the real game.

## G2: novelty over engine state, as stated, fails

- The claim was that novelty over live scripts, their wait offsets, reachable objects and a camera bucket would keep
  the trial that opened exit A's dialog, which tested facts alone had dropped.
- It did not keep that trial. An earlier trial in the same step reached the same dialog, and that one was kept. So
  the state is kept; the named trial is not.
- The kept rate was 0.61%, inside the bound. Reachable objects and the camera added nothing, because play8's archive
  already held them all. Every gain came from live units and wait offsets.
- As a driver, the same novelty did poorly. A breadth search under it reached room 63 from 2 of 25 room-49 entries.
  Most searches ran out of new stops, because a walk click is new under no kind.
- The check found the 0.61% is mostly the same states found again step after step. Carried across steps, it would be
  about 0.17%.

## What follows

- By the plan's rules, the checkpoint ladder stands, with play8 at its first rung.
- The engine-first inversion is not built.
- The rule that would have built the minimal change's fixes needed both G1 and G2 to pass, so it does not apply.
- What the gates support is narrower: the engine can turn plans into clicks, and the ladder is right on one branch of
  the opening. Novelty over engine state, as a driver, is not supported.
