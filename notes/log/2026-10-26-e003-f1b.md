## What was built

Branch `f1b`: main (e926bd2) with `fr1-f1-probe` merged (the cells, the machine switch `input_script`, the reader's
clicks), then reader switch `sentence_queue`, which needs `input_script`. The rule, with its engine lines, is
fix-round1.md's F1b section.

- The queue is four facts: `queue` (0 empty, 1 runs at the next checkAndRunSentenceScript, 2 held by a live,
  unfrozen sentence script) and `queue:verb`, `queue:a`, `queue:b`.
- A click's record is its frame up to checkAndRunSentenceScript. doSentence(STOP) writes `queue` 0. A push writes the
  three values without a fork: a value the path did not write is a copy of the fact it read (one conditional write
  per value). Where the record ends, the push is 1 or 2 by checkAndRunSentenceScript's hold test. A record that leaves
  the sentence script stopped or frozen turns 2 into 1, by a conditional write.
- The sentence event (kind `queued`): per sentence script, its two variants (idle, live and frozen), per verb, A and
  B of today's sentence inputs. It needs `queue` 1 or 2 and the three values, pops the queue and runs the sentence
  script. Every other record needs `queue` 0 or 2.
- A slot verb is clicked on the verb screen from y 144 (the least SetScreen bottom under 200) to 199. The probe had
  200 to 199.
- `reader-port read --count-only` counts the records instead of keeping them, with a time and memory watchdog;
  `--packed` streams them into taskcore's file. The kitchen with F1b kept in memory passed the 4 GB limit.

## Why it grows

1. **The clicks.** Each of the bar's cells gives about 2,400 records for a left click and 2,900 for a right one. The
   click's frame splits on the cursor and the hover: with VAR_CURSORSTATE <= 0, or the hover not at its wait, the
   hover does nothing that frame. VAR_CURSORSTATE and VAR_USERPUT are separate facts, so clicks with the cursor off
   and input on stay in the reading. It splits on the verb (script 12 compares Var[107] with 10 constants), on
   Var[108] where the hover does not write it (local 202 compares it with 316, global 4 uses it as a number), on
   Var[109] and Var[110], on Var[183] (the hover's VerbOps(Var[183]) uses it as a verb number: 143,343 forks), and on
   the cook's room and x. 211,233 paths were cut by the path limit; 254 runs past it could not be split.
2. **The queued sentences.** 1,704 jobs give 239,182 records, about 140 each. Today 28,112 sentence records came from
   about 1,850 jobs, about 15 each. Today's sentence input pins the room and A's place; the queued sentence does not,
   since the click's record holds those. So the sentence script forks on the room (9,571 forks), owners and
   Bit[5 + ego] (91,817 forks).
3. **The sentence script's later turns.** 65,501 records against 5,203: the queued sentences leave more distinct
   lists of locals.
4. **The passes.** The sentence variables' domains grow with what paths write: 132 values after pass 1, 13 after
   pass 2 (350,715 records in pass 1, 527,449 in pass 3). One click on room 33 gives 44 records with pass 1's domains
   and 561 in the whole reading.

The first build forked on `queue` where a record lets a held sentence run (591,207 records, 69,322 such forks). A
conditional write replaced the fork; the numbers above are with it.

## Why no plan

Relaxed reachability over the packed file from s17 (no deletes): no reachable action queues the door (`queue:a` 316)
or starts local 203, and room 41 is never reached. The 40 actions that write `queue:a` 316 are the sentence script's
later turns, which need a list of locals no reachable action sets. In the door cell's click run, the depth-first order
takes the cursor off or the hover not running first, and the path limit cuts the paths where both are as at s17.
