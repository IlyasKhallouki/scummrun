# 2026-10-31: E004 reductions 3 and 2, forward reachability in the reader and pass 2 reuse

Question: can the reader skip what taskcore's step (d) throws away, and write the same records? Proofs:
`experiments/E004-fast-exact/reach.md`, `pass2-reuse.md`. Numbers: `experiments/E004-fast-exact/results/reach-*`.

## The rule

The reader keeps a monotone set of reached fact values, seeded from the start. A job whose precondition does not meet the
set waits, and so does each branch of a fork. A written record's effects grow the set, by (d)'s rule. What a new value
frees runs in the next batch, again from the job's start, following only the forks its guide agrees with. A branch can
be cut because a run's merge signature holds the whole precondition, so paths with different fact values never merge.

## Exactness

`reach_check.py` compares the actions (d) alone leaves of each file.

| scope | (d) keeps of full | --reach writes | (d) keeps, not written | written, (d) does not keep | fact values reached by (d) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| kitchen | 27,633 | 27,617 | 17 | 8 | the same |
| bar | 26,203 | 26,185 | 11 | 0 | the same |
| jail | 690 | 683 | 0 | 0 | the same |
| k = 1 | 57,456 | 51,371 | 6,666 | 588 | 9 facts differ |

Each compared without a list's {0} on `args:X` and a write of 0 there (reach.md, "Lists").

Three classes separate the two.

1. **Event naming.** (d) treats a slot's place and its list as separate facts. An event whose (place, list) pair only an
   unkept record writes has its precondition met in (d). The reader never learns the event exists. No plan from the start
   reaches that pair (Lemma 2). Kitchen 11 records, bar 11, jail 0. All bar and kitchen ones are `script:2@759`.
2. **A guess over a domain only unkept records widened.** k = 1 only. In pass 1 of the full reading a record writes
   var:32 = 209, which widens the domain. 246 kept records guess var:32 over the whole domain, so (d) reaches 209, which
   frees the input that local 209 needs, and from there the record that writes 209 is itself reachable. The
   justification is circular. Without running the code the start cannot reach, a reader cannot know 209 exists. It is the
   only domain value of k = 1 that differs. This class accounts for 5,400 of the 6,666.
3. **A job read otherwise.** A run over the path limit is split in the full reading, and not with `--reach`, where it stays
   under the limit. Kitchen: `sentence(8,574)` and its frozen copy, 6 records against 8. Bar: none. k = 1: 276 against
   342, under the same labels, mostly `sentence(7,442)[inv]` and `sentence(8,442)[inv]` and their frozen copies.

With classes 1 and 2 applied to the full reading (the closure with event naming, each guess cut to `--reach`'s domains),
what is left is 0 against 0 on the bar and the jail, 6 against 8 on the kitchen and 276 against 342 on k = 1. Not all 276 were
traced one by one: the one traced outside the sentence jobs, `event(local:202@3249)`, differs by a merge the split changes.

The kitchen's optimal cost is 7 from both readings. The bar door's optimal cost was not reached: A* with landmark cost
partitioning ran out of 3 GB on both readings at f = 5, the initial value on both. lama-first plans 6 steps from both. The
unfolded task from the two bar readings differs by exactly class 1's 11 actions.

## A start with a new value

taskcore reads `NAME.reached` next to the packed file. A start holding a value outside it stops `sas` with exit code 7, and
a `serve` request gets `error`. `Builder::reach_extend` commits the new values as a round and goes on. On room 33 a first
start then a second, the second then the first, and both from the beginning give the same 676 records (670 from the
first alone). `reader-scope --reach-also FILE` reads from both on the command line. On the bar with bit:6 = 1 in a start
file: `sas` exits 7 and names it, `serve` answers `error`, and the reading from both starts has 28,890 actions against
26,185, after which `sas` runs from that start.

### What grows: `dialog_line(120..124)[202@85]`

This is the dialog line job with var:32 (the verb script) = 202 in room 85. The reader pairs every value of var:32
with every scope room, and under the delete relaxation var:32 = 202 and room = 85 are each reached. The five jobs
(verbs 120 to 124) make the same run:

- Round 4: 273 paths, 270 records, 0 cut, 0 splits. 92,611 forks on reads, 289,983 forks on non-fact tests, 116,580
  non-fact tests forgotten at merges.
- Fork keys: var:33 11,340 times; script:2 10,080; bit:19, bit:453, var:111 and var:38 5,040 each; classes 13 and 5
  of 13 objects, 1,890 each (49,140 forks on classes in all).
- Held branches: 520,380, of which 2,283 are distinct.
- One job took 241 s on 1 thread at nice 19 beside run A, 342 to 360 s in A, 524 to 530 s in D and 799 to 806 s
  in C (3 threads, load 4 to 12, 10 to 12 and 13 to 16).
- Round 5 runs them again from their held branches: 120 records each, 179 to 275 s each.
- The five jobs set round 4's wall: two waves on 3 threads, 530 s and 402 s in D, against 931 s for the round.

The chain, from the IR:

1. Room 85's local 202 moves an actor in a loop and calls global 114 when the ego is near.
2. Global 114 calls `loadRoom(49)`.
3. That runs room 85's exit, which reads bit:453 and starts global 17, then room 49's entry, which reads bit:19.

Room 49 is in Part I's scope and not in k = 2's, where the path leaves the scope at that load. On k = 2 under the
same switches the same jobs each take under 1 s. So the scope makes the run grow.

What does not cause it:
- **value_sets (F12):** without it the job was still running after about 940 s on 1 thread.
- **The held list:** keeping each branch once cut the held branches 228 times. The peak RSS was the same.

What makes the job take 241 to 806 s, against at most 53 s for any job of the VM's attempt 2, is not
separated. Candidates are the path limit, which cut and dropped paths before split_limit, and `--reach`'s held
branches and guides.

Memory: RSS rises in a sawtooth while three of these runs go, from 0.8 to 2 GB, and peaks at 3.4 GB at their end. That
is about 1.1 GB per run, held by the machine's state of the run, not by its held branches.

### The two stops: `dialog_line(120)[204@28]`

Room 28's local 204 run as the verb script. In the scope, var:53 (3 values) and var:196 (5 values) are facts, and
var:164 is not. Its writes in the scope include constants, `getActorX`, `VAR_VIRT_MOUSE_X` and VAR_VERB_SCRIPT, so
it has no domain.

- **Run A:** split on var:53 (3 parts), then on var:196. `--reach` keeps 1 of its 5 values, the ones it already held,
  so that part is the same run and still 1 path over the limit. With no fact left, split_limit stops the reading.
- **Run D, with grow_limit:** the part is read whole at a limit of 4,096 and cuts nothing. 3 of its paths end at the
  step limit (5,000 steps), and split_limit stops the reading.

The unit loops `Local[1] = 330 + 3 * Local[0]; setState(Local[1], 1); Local[0]++` until `Local[0] > Var[164]`. With
var:164 unknown, each pass forks on a non-fact test, and one path never leaves the loop. No path limit and no step
limit lifts this. The loop ends only if the reading bounds var:164 (room 28's entry writes 8 to it).

This stop is what split_limit is for. Without the switch, as in the VM's attempt 2, such paths end at a limit and are
dropped with no record.

### Changes, each behind a switch or with the same output

- `reader-scope --switches S,...` gives fix round 1's reader switches by name.
- `grow_limit` (off by default): a run past the limit with no fact left to split on is read whole with the limit 4
  times larger, up to 16,384. Exact, because the limit only ends paths. `tests/grow_limit.rs`.
- Under `--reach`, each held branch of a run is kept once, where it was first held. The waits were already a set, so
  the output is the same. The bar's packed sha1s are unchanged, 00960d7d and 1da422ac (with fix round 1's switches).
- `READER_TRACE=1`: splits, job starts, runs of 1 s or more with their counters, and jobs of 1 s or more, on stderr.
  With it unset, nothing is written.

### Projection

The reading cannot finish while var:164 has no bound, because split_limit stops it at round 5. The numbers below
assume that stop is lifted.

- **If the rest has k = 2's shape under `--reach`:** there, pass 1's first five rounds were 8% of the reading's job
  seconds. Part I's first five rounds are at least 3,800 job seconds in D: about 2,400 in round 4, and about 1,400
  in round 5 up to the stop, mostly its five dialog jobs and eight 15 to 20 s events of local 202 at 422. That projects to about
  48,000 job seconds, or 6,000 s wall on 8 threads used fully, at D's per-job speed (load 10 to 12).
- **If the 202@85 jobs rerun often:** this projection does not hold. They hold 1,170 to 2,283 distinct branches. Each
  round that frees one reruns the five jobs at 180 to 275 s each, which is one wave on 8 threads. k = 2 had 1,027
  rounds.
- **Not measured:** taskcore, state:316 = 1, Part I's key goals and lama-first, because the reading did not finish.
