# 2026-11-01: E003 F1c's gate with --reach on both sides

Question: with `--reach` on both sides, does the kitchen's reading with input_settled stay within 3 times the same switches
without it? Is global 4's Bit[547] then read only at its start value? Is `--reach` exact there?

Answer: the gate passes. Over seven pairs the reading takes a median 1.58 times the CPU time and 1.55 times the wall time, with
1.47 times the records, and taskcore's operators grow 2.45 times. Bit[547] is read only at its start value 0. `--reach` keeps
the same fact values as (d) on the full reading. The optimal cost was not settled: it is 6 or 7 on both readings. Numbers in
`experiments/E003-path-through-cloud/results/f1c-gate-reach.log`.

## The gate

Laptop, nice 10, 3 threads, seed 1, the kitchen from s17. Both sides have value_sets, draw_clears, actor_rooms, split_limit and
reach, with `--elsewhere --live-locals`. The first pair, at load 9.5:

| reading | records | wall s | CPU s | peak MB | operators (s17) | lama-first |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| reach | 27,739 | 9.2 | 22.6 | 535 | 17,672 | plan, 10 steps |
| reach, input_settled | 40,765 | 14.2 | 38.7 | 655 | 43,323 | plan, 10 steps |
| ratio | 1.47 | 1.55 | 1.71 | 1.22 | 2.45 | |

Other jobs kept the load between 8 and 17, and the same reading took between 9 and 55 s. So the two readings were run one after
the other seven times. CPU ratios: 1.55 to 2.40, median 1.58. Wall ratios: 1.09 to 8.20, median 1.55. The two wall ratios above 3
(8.20 and 3.17) are runs where the input_settled reading had under one core on average: 0.69 and 0.86 CPU s per wall s, against
2.72 in the first pair.

Without `--reach` the same readings were 71,242 and 475,441 records, 80.8 and 435.7 CPU s, 17,712 and 44,839 operators
(F1c's gate). In the first pair, input_settled adds 16 CPU s with `--reach`; without it, it added 355. The operators are nearly
those without `--reach` (43,323 against 44,839): taskcore's reductions already removed what `--reach` does not read.

lama-first finds the meat goal, owner:566 = ego, from both. With input_settled the door step is `fold:walk_to(316)[202]`, the fold of
actorroom:6 = -1 and = 28, both with bit:547 = {0} and the hover at its wait. The rest of the plan is the same 9 steps as without it,
where the door step is `walk_to(316)`. The plan was not played.

## Bit[547]

Under `--reach`, bit:547 is reached at 0 only, its value in s17. No action of either file writes bit:547 or has bit:547 = {1} in its
precondition, and none reads var:182. So at global 4's first test only the bit:547 = 0 side runs. The other side, and var:182's fork
under it, is held and never freed. This is the reason the factor of 3 is gone, as predicted.

No record writes bit:547 because the writes that make bit: a fact, indexed Bit writes whose index does not fold, are unknown-target
writes in the machine: left out of the record's effects and listed on it. That holds with or without `--reach`.

The cook's factor shrinks too. actorroom:6 is reached at -1, 0 and 28, not 72. sentence(7,320)[202] has 30 actions: 12 at -1,
12 at 28, 6 at 0.

What input_settled still adds: 10,548 sentence and walk actions with var:32 = 202 (the bar's local input script) and 1,320 with
var:32 = 14. The plain value's 3,274 and the inventory's 570 are the reach reading's 3,844.

## Exactness

`reach_check.py` on the full reading with input_settled (442,665 actions, the same file as F1c's gate) against the `--reach` one
(39,661 actions), from s17:

- (d) keeps 39,677 of the full file. 17 of them are not written: 11 by event naming (Lemma 2, `event(script:2@759...)`) and 6 from a
  job read otherwise (`sentence(8,574)` and its frozen copy). 8 written actions are not kept by (d), all that same job.
- The fact values (d) reaches are the same on every fact both files have, 5,865 values. 87 facts are only in the full file, each
  with one value.
- The reader's `.reached` equals (d)'s on its own file. No guess was cut.

These are the classes and counts of the kitchen without input_settled (17 and 8).

Taskcore's task with all reductions: 13,905 actions and 43,323 operators from `--reach`, 13,907 and 44,839 from the full file.

The optimal cost from s17 was not settled. A* with landmark cost partitioning starts at h0 = 6 on both tasks and did not close
f = 6 in 1,800 s, at 1.43 GB on each. Without input_settled the same search gives h0 = 7 and a plan of cost 7 after 8 expansions.
A greedy search with bound 8 finds a 7-step plan on both, the same steps: `event(local:211@33)`, `walk_to(316)[202]`, the sentence
script, local 218, `sentence(9,566)`, the sentence script twice. So the optimum is 6 or 7 on both. A search for a cost-6 plan with
greedy FF found none in 600 s, which proves nothing. Stronger admissible searches were not available here: the FD build has no LP
solver, and LM-cut and the stubborn-set prunings do not support conditional effects.

## Off: unchanged

Every switch off, `reader-port read kitchen --packed`: 72,256 records, 71,729 actions, sha1 279b7a24 (main's), and the reading equals
golden.py's (72,258 lines, 0 differences). The only code change is reader-port's `--no-keep`, which keeps no records when the packed
file is streamed. It gives the same packed and `.reached` files at about half the peak memory, and made the full reading fit in
311 MB instead of 4.2 GB.
