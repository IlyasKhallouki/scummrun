# 2026-11-04: E014, why the demo's plans failed, and the run after the fixes

The last run (play7) never left the first room. The model planned there, and the engine refuted the plans. This
note covers the diagnosis, the fixes, and the next run (amendment (8) of `experiments/E014-fate-demo/plan.md`).

## Why the plans failed

**Effect mismatch (38% of play7's try wall).** Almost all of it was one plan: walk to an object, which turns the
cursor off. The plan was right, and the engine did what the record said. The matcher was wrong.

- When the player already stands at the object, the sentence script does not wait for the walk. It runs straight on
  and starts the object's script, which runs at once, nested, and parks at its first break.
- Before the next decision point, the engine's next frame runs that object script once more.
- The matcher compared the record with the state at the end of the step. By then the object script had moved on, so
  its arguments no longer matched the record, and the plan was refuted.

Widening was not to blame: the reading made without it has the same record. Nothing was learned from the
refutation either. The same plan was tried from nine entries, each try taking 2-3 minutes.

**Model-blind (14%).** The reader had no rule for sound commands. Any path through one ended and was dropped. An
ambient sound script parks just after such a command, so no reading ever reached the position the engine was in. The
demo's scripts hold 275 sound commands, so every path that plays a sound was missing from the model.

Two smaller fixes went in with these:

- actors standing off the screen are no longer offered as click points;
- a refinement that loses the target's fact now stops as a refinement, not as a planner error.

The 80-try falsifier became a wall limit, since 80 tries now take only 8 minutes. Monkey Island's checks all pass
with every switch on.

## The run

On the cloud VM with 10 workers, from the boot. It stopped after 502 tries in 1.5 hours, when the disk filled.

- **Rooms:** it entered a second room, by choosing its way through the opening dialog. 316 of the 628 entries are
  there. Every entry replays from boot with 0 differences.
- **Plans:** the model's plans reached their targets 46 times (play7: 0). Effect mismatch fell to 0.3% of the try
  wall and model-blind to 1.9%.
- **Not reached:** the demo's end. Nothing went into the inventory.
- **Cost:** 1.85 h of VM, $2.84.

## What bound it now

- **The readings got big.** Once the reader reads past sound commands, the world-map room's scripts run on, and the
  interface's hover script runs every hovered object's script. One scope's reading went from 4.5 minutes to more than
  25 on the laptop. On the VM, readings hit the 5-minute bound in 177 tries (65% of the try wall), 14 ran out of
  memory, and they filled the disk.
- **The executor still uses Monkey Island's interface numbers.** It waits for Monkey Island's walk-to verb before it
  walks. In this game that verb is a different one, so every pick-up sentence, and every walk to an object off the
  screen, never forms an input. That is why nothing reached the inventory (40 tries).
- **Nothing is learned across tries.** The cursor-off target was reached 26 times and kept being chosen.
