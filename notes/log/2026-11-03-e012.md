# 2026-11-03: E012, the game's own mechanics, integrated

E012 adds a layer to the explorer that splits each step by who decides it. The player's moves are planned on the
reading; the game's moves (time passing, random draws) are played by the engine; where the model cannot act, the
explorer tries the inputs the interface offers; the game's own end marks a move dead. Nine components built it on
their own branches, each switch off by default. This note is their merge: the gates on the merged build, and what the
layer costs on E011's 15 kitchen tries. Plan and amendments: `experiments/E012-game-mechanics/plan.md`; numbers:
`experiments/E012-game-mechanics/results/integration/`. Seed 1 only.

## The merge

The components touched different files except plan.md and one doc line, so the merge needed no code change between
them. Each component's own files are its final head's, byte for byte.

One fix came from outside the components: E011's verify of an archive entry (`fmi explore verify`, and the driver's
verify of a goal entry) never worked. It booted the engine to the first checkpoint's frame, then asked it to run to
that same frame, and it never scheduled the movie's later events. The afford and wait components found it. Fixed, it
gives explore.py's own record on E009's door entry: 6 checkpoints, 0 differences, the door open.

## Exact, with every switch off and every switch on

- **Off**, the 15 tries equal round 2's in every stop, frame, input and entry. That holds in the fast settings,
  against round 2 run beside it, round 2's published run and the Python reference, and in the reference settings.
- **On**, every attempt stays the same, and the layer adds 33 entries: 18 stops of the wait, 15 kept affordance
  inputs. All 76 new entries verify from boot with 0 differences.
- E011's gates G1-G12 pass on the merged build. The engine runs equal svm-w3b's dumps with the end report switch off
  and on. Forks stay exact under load: 594,383 load comparisons, 0 differ.
- Every component's gate passes again on the merged build. The waits stop at the cook's door at exactly the slot's
  due frame from all 13 room-28 entries. The forced delay forks choose the least matching delay. The kept affordance
  outcomes verify from boot.

Two gates read differently than E011 recorded them:

- **The Python reference's test (a)** gives the same route and line, but with 503 window children in two runs where
  E011 recorded 501 or 502. Its strict window search runs children in a thread pool and stops when the bound reaches
  the best result so far, so the number of children in flight at the stop depends on timing. The two extra children
  came after the best, and every child present in both runs has the same outcome. A third run, on a quieter laptop,
  gave 502.
- **G9 with the layer on** is not a comparison with the reference any more. Selection over T changes the cells by
  design, and the replay doubles have no reading to compute T from, so the try refuses to run. G9 passes with the
  layer off, as round 2 ran it.

## What the layer costs on the 15 tries

Round 2 and the merged build with everything on, alternating try by try on the same load:

- **In all:** 183.8 s against 191.8 s. The median try is 3.2 s against 4.4 s.
- **The reading** in fmi's process with its store resident saves 23.2 s. The fresh ring(38) readings take 0.5-3.1 s
  instead of 1.9-4.7 s, and a refinement from another entry 7.5 s instead of 19.7 s.
- **The layer's own steps** add 9.1 s. T is 1.8-2.1 s per ring(33) reading. Each try's affordance step at its R0
  stop is 0.3-0.7 s. The wait costs what the idle measurement it replaces did.
- **Planning** took 5.9 s longer for the same requests and plans. This is not explained yet.

With a third resident process beside them, the merged build's planning took 9 s more again (63.6 s against 54.4 s).
The harness keeps a reading's planner across a refinement only while MemAvailable is at least 7 GB. With three
processes it hovered at 7.1-8.5 GB, and the merged process is up to 0.8 GB larger (the resident store, T). Below the
threshold the planner was closed and built again: one request took 2.5 s instead of 0.07 s.

## What the tries did

In all 15 tries the attempt stops on Lemma R's R0 in a dialog, so the affordance step ran 15 times:

- Each step tried the dialog's 4 lines and kept one new state.
- The waits were static at 6 entries and stopped twice at the other 9, when the dialog's lines appeared or the room
  changed.
- No delay fork triggered: no window's records check a draw in rooms 33 and 38, where the game draws once per frame.
- No move ended the game.

The goal was never a target, so the goal-again step did not run. The kitchen rerun is the first run that can exercise
it.

## The driver with every switch on

Four jobs from the boot, with the kitchen check's settings, ran the paths no gate covers end to end:

- the archive's novelty went to each try in its job file, and T grew from the goal's one atom to 576;
- the tries' affordance and checkpoint entries were ingested into cells over T;
- the fixed verify confirmed all 13 new entries from boot.

## Not in the merged build

The due-frame window from section 1 is not built. Its job is to give a paused slot's planned event its exact due
frame in the window search, and no component took it.
