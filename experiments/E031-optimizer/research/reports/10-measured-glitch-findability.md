# Measured: how findable each glitch is

Measurements made for E031's estimate (2026-11-16). Kind: measured, derived, or could-not-measure. Evaluation only: nothing here is an input of the optimizer.

## Notes

Everything here is evaluation on the hand-routed reference (runs/anypercent/m2n). Nothing measured is meant as optimizer input.

What the numbers say about findability

- Near certain by a general scan: the logo glitch (one key, 47-frame window, signal in Status v19, pays from the next frame) and luck by delay (the random state moves every frame at menus and on about half the frames in the catacombs; the outcome shows within one step). Both need the judge to count ticks, and luck needs a look-ahead of one step, because a fixed continuation survives a delay only 3-16% of the time.
- Already found, improvable: the early safe opening. It has no frame window. The lever is the entry frame: keeper away on 33% of entry frames, and the sentence count ranges 4-16.
- Findable with a staged scan: the slide state in the store. Window 20 frames at talkspeed 85 (5 at 255), 2 of 72 verb-slot pairs, 4.6% of floor points, no Esc needed. It needs automatic skips off. Its signal is only the input counter (Status up/cur, vars 52/53), so a progress-based novelty test is blind to it.
- Hard: every use of the slide. Each is a chain of 3-4 separately timed inputs with windows of 1-4 frames on at least one link (monkey reading 2 frames, its Esc 3; feather click 2; the reading's slot click before the head dialogue 1; head sentence 2), plus small click targets for the oars (3% and 6% of the screen). The first large saving comes 73 goals and 127 s after the setup, and the setup costs time first.
- The carry is long, not narrow: 27 needed inputs of 85, 13 frame-exact, but most points tolerate the rest being delayed 1-3 frames.

Things the plan should take from this

- Windows were measured with the other reference inputs fixed. Where later inputs are frame-tied this understates the true window (store floor click: 1 frame fixed, 7-20 frames once the Esc moves with it or is left out).
- The detector 'input counter above what the cutscene depth implies' fires on 26% of the reference's frames and on none of full5's before its end flag; its one false alarm is the map page (1 frame).
- 'Ego not in the room while input is on' marks the oars opportunity, but only exists once the slide is alive.
- Timing-only tricks (head variant, necklace) have no state signal at all; only arrival-frame comparison finds them.
- A blind key scan must leave out Space (pause dialog, the child hangs in real time) and must treat a dead child as an outcome (32+ fatal errors seen).

Limits

- Savings for the early safe opening, the logo glitch over a whole route, and oars against monkey separately are not measured pairs; they are documented or derived figures, marked as such.
- The blind-scan hit rate for the store (1 in 2,400) assumes the three measured marginals are independent.
- One launch was lost to the Space hang and two fight launches used a wrong win test before the third; their outputs (out/logo first attempt, fight.json fight part, fight2.json) should be ignored. The book-carry part of fight.json is valid.
- One engine that is not mine was running at the end (config under /var/tmp/fast-mi/direct/video-full5/run-audio); I left it alone. The repository is unchanged (git status clean).
