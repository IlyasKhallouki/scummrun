# The audit of the calculated estimate (2026-11-16)

**Did the first version hold?** no: it was corrected in place

## The odds after the fixes

Beating 21:01.483 (track 6 removed, parameters drawn from their ranges): 0. Not one pair in 150,000 x 150,000 draws in any of the four strata, at correlation rho 0, 0.5 or 0.9. That was also the result before the fixes. The new median is 1:01:04.8 without track 6 and 1:03:19.9 with it (was 53:57 with it); 10th to 90th percentile 46:39 to 1:37:04 with track 6. Under 60:00: 44.6% with track 6, 48.4% without; under 30:00 without track 6: 0.0012%. The target appears only when nearly every assumed parameter sits at its optimistic end at once: 2.18% with independent stages, 7.8% at rho 0.5, 13.2% at rho 0.9. The owner's 'much higher than 10%' needs the optimistic corner plus strong correlation, or an unknown gain of about 1,400 s (10th percentile) to 2,400 s (median) beyond everything modelled. With 1,200 s of unknown gain found for certain, the odds are 3.8%. The assistant's first guess of about 10% is also far above what these numbers support. Within the model the answer is under 1%; the remaining uncertainty is structural (see doubts), not in the Monte Carlo.

## What was fixed

- q_delay was never measured: the old 7/8 ('a working delay at 6 of 6 points') counted d=0 as a working delay, but d=0 is the reference's own timing and works by construction. Without it, delays 1..12 work at 2 of 6 points: none of the fight choices 485-488, and catacomb exits at d=5 and d=7-10 (gf/out/fight3.json, late.json). q_delay is now a measured range 0.375 / 0.59 / 0.83. At the centre, P(every draw point solved again) falls from 73.8% to 39%.
- The direct-walk pool counted idle frames twice. It assumed only 6 idle frames per step (busy share 0.929). Joining full5's frame log with path.pkl, the 403 no-gain room-change steps (34,181 frames, confirmed) hold 9,707 idle frames, a busy share of 0.716, and re-timing already removes that idle. The pool falls from 1,974 s to 1,503 s (1,124 s if direct walks carried no idle at all).
- b_inroom (assumed 0.3-0.6) was applied to frames that already had padding removed. The measured busy share of those frames is 0.653 (all 377 in-room no-gain steps) to 0.702 (205 in visits with no gain). It is now an MR; the drop pool rises from 683 s to 1,158 s. Fixes 2 and 3 nearly cancel in seconds, but drops are harder to realise, so the detour removed at the centre falls from 76.3% to 71.7%.
- R and G were claimed to share nothing, but two stages serve both sides. The delay scan serves R's draw points and store entry and G's luck. The walk stage serves R's e_walk and G's walking beyond the hand route. The script now mixes four strata (scan built or not, walk stage built or not), with R and G independent within each. Without the scan, draw points fall back to the frozen search and Credit Early's store entry is kept with probability 1-(41/61)(1-q_search). e_cut now requires the executor and drops.
- The store entry was counted twice: once in Credit Early and again as one of n_dp's draw points. n_dp is now 7/11/19.
- pen_draw was charged once however many draw points failed. It now scales by the expected count of failed points, given at least one.
- G was subtracted even in draws where the re-timer failed and the route stays as full5 played it. Those draws now get G = 0 for the percentiles; the odds are unaffected because those draws are above 3 h.
- The feather hold used a blind joint scan of 423,522 tries, inconsistent with how the model treats the other chained inputs. It now uses sig_keep, the same step-by-step counter mechanism as the carry. p_feather moves from 16.5% to 24.5%.
- The upper ends of e_walk (0.9), e_cut (0.8) and e_tune (0.8) were below hand quality, so even the best case could not reach the hand route. They are now 1.0 with centres unchanged. This is the main reason the all-optimistic odds of 21:01.483 rose from 0.069% to 2.18%.
- Added table 9b, which correlates the success draws through a common factor (Gaussian copula, rho 0 / 0.5 / 0.9) because one team building on one codebase does not fail independently. P(all found) for the weighted stratum is computed by quadrature.
- Text: the atom-changing no-news steps are 279-72 = 207, not 205. estimate.md was rewritten with an Audit section and the new output pasted; the pre-audit originals are in experiments/E031-optimizer-audit/ (estimate.orig.py, estimate.orig.md, out.orig.txt).
- Verified correct: every segment value (17 segments x 3 routes, plus the splits against the no-slide route) against tstruct/segments.json; waiting inside extra visits (re-ran rep4.py: 3,714.6 s); the waiting pools (sum 5,526.3 s); the 403 steps / 34,181 frames from path.pkl; both hand anchors reproduced (1,681.30 s and 1,261.37 s); and the Monte Carlo itself (a weighted all-found stratum plus plain draws that missed something is an unbiased estimate of R's distribution, and the sampler matches the plain function to 1e-6 s).

## Doubts that remain

- The 0 is mostly set by the model's structure, not its data. R cannot go below the no-slide hand route's structure, and G is capped at the hand-known gains plus at most 57 s of walking. So beating 21:01.483 means matching the slide route's structure and execution almost everywhere. A route order better than the hand route, or a glitch nobody has found, is outside the model (table 13 sizes how big such a gain would have to be).
- The pool sizes 279 steps / 16,808 frames (drops) and 13,280 direct-walk frames come from a graph report I could not find among the files. My own joins give pools of the same size, but with different step counts (205 and 377 against 279).
- q_delay's low end uses a fixed continuation through later draws, which is strict. With a re-solved continuation the true value may be higher; one 13-delay scan per fight choice with a searched continuation would settle it. The draw-point terms (q_search, pen_draw, n_dp) are now among the five largest movers of the median.
- Most glitch-chain probabilities (p_ts, sig_keep^6, s_step, s1_monkey, cov_in_monkey, p_part2, p_order, p_regain, p_head) are guesses with no measurement behind their ranges. They are multiplied about ten deep, giving 0.0377% for the whole chain at the centre. Correlation among them is shown only as a bracket (table 9b), not estimated.
- Correlation across R and G beyond the two shared stages is not modelled; one team's competence would plausibly correlate the executor with the glitch scanner.
- The 'detour' split assumes a room's extra visits are average visits of that room, and the re-routing shares are applied equally in every segment.
- R_ART = 1 (all probe and calm idle is removable) is tagged as derived but is really an assumption. So is the claim that the intro card also shortens without track 6; it matters only in the 3-4.5% of draws without the intro skip.
- Forming the slide needs automatic skips off and a scan budget of about 29,000 tries per store visit. Whether a general scanner pays that cost at the right visits within a few laptop hours is folded into cov_floor = 1 and the pair and frame coverages, which may be optimistic.
- Raising the upper ends of e_walk, e_cut and e_tune to 1 (hand quality) is a judgement call: it was needed to make the best case able to reach the hand route, but no measurement supports either 0.8-0.9 or 1.
