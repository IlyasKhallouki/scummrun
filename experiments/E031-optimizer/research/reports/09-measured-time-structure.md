# Measured: the time structure, segment by segment

Measurements made for E031's estimate (2026-11-16). Kind: measured, derived, or could-not-measure. Evaluation only: nothing here is an input of the optimizer.

## Notes

All numbers are in experiments/E031-optimizer/research/segments/segments.json (one row per segment and route, with the splits against both references). The scripts are in the same folder: path.py, facts_tree.py, ref_measure.py, ref_facts.py, cuts.py, cuts2.py, load.py, seg.py, rep1.py to rep5.py. "<tstruct>" in the entries means that folder. No project file was changed.

Main result: full5 is 9.0 times the 21:01 route at bit 565 (10,965 s against 1,221 s). The excess is 56% waiting, 36% extra room visits and 8% everything else. Walking inside the visits both routes make is only 249 s over the reference, so walk tuning is a small lever; the count of visits (609 against 218 or 241) and the waiting are the large ones.

Definitions
- Clock: seconds are quarter-frames / 239.992156448, the reference's clock. Class splits use main-loop ticks; 0.8 to 1.3 s per route lies outside the main loop and is not classed.
- Routes: "slide" is the 21:01.483 route (track 6 absent). The 23:11.320 route equals it except for +129.84 s in S16. "No-slide" is the 28:01.4 route (track 6 present). The two hand routes are the same movie up to frame 4,212, so S00 to S03 are equal.
- Start: the references start at the logo-skip key (frame 46), full5 at boot, because it presses no key. From the logo room's own end (frame 961) full5's S00 would be 167.7 s.
- Room visits: maximal runs of one room in the frame log, cut at segment ends, room 0 left out.
- Progress stops: end-path stops that add a key to the cumulative news or a new object (257, the earlier report's count).

Limits
- Cut points: the rung-based cuts in Part I (S01, S04, S06) are consistent on the rung keys only; the routes hold different other objects there. The cuts at S02, S03, S05, S07 and all from S08 on are also consistent on every object the three routes share. No finer consistent cut exists inside S11 for all three routes.
- The detour split assumes a room's extra visits are average visits of that room; the ranges in the entries show how far that can move.
- The context's figure "45% waiting" counts only the gap before a trial's first input. Idle frames in all are 50.4% of the boot-to-end time, because 1,254 s more lies between the clicks of one trial.

Engine use: two reference replays (62 s and 88.5 s) and six `high9 verify` runs, one at a time at nice 10. The references were replayed with their own seed through the October Python controller, for evaluation only.
